Aster 0.7.0 moves the settings into a tab of their own with a page list, and makes profiles easier to arrange and open.

## New

- Settings open as a tab. A list of pages on the left (Application, Appearance, Profiles, Terminal, Clipboard and mouse, Search, Docked window, Keyboard shortcuts, SSH) shows one page at a time; searching still looks through every page. Switch pages with a click or Ctrl+PageUp and Ctrl+PageDown. Tab shortcuts keep working while the settings are open, and switching away and back keeps the page and the search where they were. Ctrl+, or Esc closes the settings tab.
- The Application page holds the language, the Explorer menu and buttons to open the log folder, open the config file and report a problem, with the version of Aster under the page list.
- Profiles have a group field. Own profiles with a group get their own section in the profile selector.
- Ctrl+Enter in the profile selector opens the chosen profile in a split to the right instead of a new tab.
- "Text padding" sets the space between the text and the edges of a pane.
- The profile editor lays its fields out in two columns when they do not fit the height of the window.
