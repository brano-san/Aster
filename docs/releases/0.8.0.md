Aster 0.8.0 is about colors: a settings page that shows every color scheme with its palette, and a color scheme of its own for any profile.

## New

- A Color scheme page in the settings. It holds the theme mode and the dark and light scheme settings, and lists every scheme, your own first, with its 16 colors and text color on its background. Enter or a click applies a scheme to the current mode, the scheme in use is marked "Current", and opening the page jumps to it. Searching finds schemes by name from any page.
- Profiles can have a color scheme of their own. Set "Color scheme" in the profile editor; the panes of that profile are drawn with it while the rest of the window keeps the window scheme. The name is checked against the known schemes.

## Changed

- Closing a pane on Linux now hangs up the whole process group of the shell. Programs that ignore the hang-up are asked to terminate after a second and are killed two seconds later, without making the window wait.
