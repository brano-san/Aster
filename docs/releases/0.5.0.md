Aster 0.5.0 is about tabs, panes and selection: reopening closed tabs, typing into many panes at once, tab activity and progress, tab colors and a question before closing running programs.

## New

- Reopen closed tab brings back the last closed tabs, up to ten, with their splits, folders and recent output.
- Switch to the last used tab, from the command palette or a shortcut of your own.
- Alt+Shift+arrows move the divider next to the active pane.
- "Panes to separate tabs" and "Combine all tabs" in the tab menu move panes between tabs without restarting them.
- Type into all panes of a tab or into all tabs at once. A bar on each pane shows it; click the bar to stop.
- Background tabs that printed something get a dot. Progress reported by programs (OSC 9;4) shows under the tab title and on the Windows taskbar.
- Tab colors from the tab menu or the command palette, or from the profile color.
- Closing a pane, tabs or the window while a program still runs in it asks first and lists the programs. Turn this off with "Confirm closing running programs".
- Alt-drag selects a block of text, Shift-click extends the selection and Shift-click also works in programs that capture the mouse. Double-click word boundaries follow the "word-separator" setting.
- "Focus follows the mouse" setting for split panes.
